OUTCOME
The share of the fund (0-100%) allocated to each profile. The slider records the peso amount given to the right-hand applicant; the left-hand applicant receives the complement. Amounts are converted to percentages, and the two shares in a task sum to 100.

ALLOCATION INEQUALITY (BLOCK D)
D_it = |share_i1t - 50|, ranging from 0 (equal split) to 50 (all to one applicant). It is one value per task; using either applicant's share gives the same value. Secondary measure: an indicator for an exact equal split (D_it = 0).

RESPONDENT MODERATORS (all measured after the conjoint module)

- Education. Survey item: education, 0-9 ("Sin estudios" ... "Posgrado"). Coding for confirmatory tests: Primary: completed tertiary education (codes 6, 7, 8, 9) = 1, otherwise 0. Robustness: 0-9 treated as continuous and standardized. "Prefiero no decir" is set to missing.

- Gender. Survey item: sexo (0 = male, 1 = female). Coding for confirmatory tests: Female = 1. Category "Otro" (99) is excluded from H9-H10 models.

- Political orientation. Survey item: posicion_politica, 0-10 (left-right). Coding for confirmatory tests: Continuous, standardized. "Independiente", "Ninguno" and "Prefiero no responder" (posicion_politica_nr) are excluded from H11-H13 models; see Exploratory analysis.

INTRA-RESPONDENT RELIABILITY
For each respondent, the shares given to the same profile in the practice task and in its repeat are compared. Two measures are computed:

- Directional consistency: whether the same profile receives more than 50% in both tasks (or exactly 50% in both). This is the analogue of the agreement rate that Clayton et al. (2026) define for binary choices.
- Continuous discrepancy: the absolute difference between the two shares given to the same profile (0-100 pp).

At the sample level we report the proportion of respondents who are directionally consistent, the mean and median discrepancy, and the correlation between the two shares.

QUALITY-CONTROL MEASURES
Comprehension check (manip_check), attention check (instructed-response item), completion time overall and on the conjoint module, slider movement in each task, careless response patterns, and the "Prefiero no responder" option in each task (cbc_qX_nr).
